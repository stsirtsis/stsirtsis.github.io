Three papers accepted at [NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc)! 🎉 We show that [AI-mediated communication can steer collective opinion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16245) and [LLM providers have a financial incentive to increase test-time compute](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21839), and we introduce a [statistical protocol for subsidizing and performing experiments sequentially](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06520).
